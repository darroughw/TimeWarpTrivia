Six decades of pop culture trivia on one shared screen. Nobody leaves this game feeling great about their memory.

Host it on the TV. Everyone else answers from their own phone's browser. No app to download, no account, just a room code and a name. 2 to 10 players, all of them about to find out how much of their childhood they've actually retained.

Pick a decade, or all six of them, from the 60s through the 2010s. Score is based on speed, so hesitating counts the same as being wrong. Whoever's in last place after the main rounds gets a bonus question to themselves, on the big screen, with everyone else watching and not helping.

The scoreboard keeps score of the whole game, not just the last round. So does everyone in the room, later, when they bring it up again.
