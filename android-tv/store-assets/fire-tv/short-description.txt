Nostalgia, weaponized.

TimeWarp Trivia turns your TV into the stage and everyone else's phone into the buzzer. Host a game on the big screen, and 2–10 players join instantly from their own phone's browser — no app to download, no account, just a room code and a name.

Filter to one decade or throw open all six: the 60s through the 2010s, plus deep-cut topics for shows and games with die-hard fans of their own. Thousands of pop-culture questions on music, movies, TV, toys, and trends test how much you actually remember versus how much you just think you remember.

Score is speed-based, so answering right isn't enough — answering right fast is what separates first place from the scoreboard's basement. And whoever's in last after the main rounds doesn't get to coast: they face a solo question, alone, with everyone else watching, before the game closes out.

Built for a shared screen and a room full of people, not a solo scroll — pass the phones around, pick your decades, and see who actually remembers the 90s versus who just says they do.
