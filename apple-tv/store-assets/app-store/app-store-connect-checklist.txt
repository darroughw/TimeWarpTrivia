APP PRIVACY ("Nutrition label")
Apple's categories differ from Google's Data Safety wording but the
underlying facts are the same app, same three surfaces (web, phone,
TV wrapper) — see the Play checklist's DATA SAFETY FORM section for the
full reasoning; summarized here in Apple's category names:

- Contact Info > Name — the chosen nickname. Same judgment call the Play
  checklist made: Apple's own guidance covers any name-like identifier a
  user provides, not just a legal name, so this counts even with no
  real-name field anywhere. Required to join/host.
- Contact Info > Email Address — only if a player uses the in-app
  feedback form and opts to include one. Optional.
- User Content > Other User Content — gameplay data (room code,
  questions shown, answers, timing, scores) and free-text feedback
  message text. Gameplay: required. Feedback text: optional.
- Diagnostics > Crash Data — Sentry. Automatic.
- Diagnostics > Performance Data, Usage Data > Product Interaction —
  Vercel Analytics & Speed Insights, PostHog gameplay events. Automatic.
- Location > Coarse Location — Yes, same finding as the Play checklist:
  app/providers.tsx's posthog.init() call still doesn't set
  disable_geoip: true as of this check, so PostHog's IP-based geoip
  enrichment applies to every event, and Vercel Analytics' geography
  breakdown works the same way. Precise Location: No — no
  navigator.geolocation call anywhere in the app. Add disable_geoip:
  true to that posthog.init() call instead if the goal is to avoid
  declaring Location at all (same fix noted, still not applied, on the
  Play side).
- Not collected: Health & Fitness, Financial Info, Sensitive Info,
  Contacts, Browsing History, Search History, Purchases — no accounts,
  no payment flow, no address book access anywhere in the app.
- "Data Used to Track You" (Apple's cross-app/site ad-tracking
  question, distinct from anything Play's form asks): No — no
  advertising, no data sharing with data brokers or ad networks, no use
  of IDFA. This is a clean No independent of the geoip/analytics answer
  above, which is first-party product analytics, not tracking.
- Linked to your identity vs. not linked: most of the above is not
  linked to a persistent identity — there's no account, and a nickname
  only exists for the lifetime of one ephemeral room. Apple's form asks
  this per data type; default to "Not Linked" unless a specific
  reviewer pushback says otherwise, since nothing here persists or
  re-identifies a player across sessions.

AGE RATING QUESTIONNAIRE
- No violence, no gambling, no in-app purchases, no unrestricted web
  access (the app never opens arbitrary URLs, only /tv on
  timewarptrivia.com). Expect 4+.
- User Generated Content: the free-text nickname field is the one
  UGC-adjacent surface (JoinScreen.tsx-equivalent web flow) — answer the
  questionnaire's UGC question honestly. Apple's Guideline 1.2 (Safety —
  User-Generated Content) specifically wants a way to filter objectionable
  content and remove abusive users for any UGC surface; both already
  exist and are worth citing if App Review asks: lib/nicknameFilter.ts
  blocks a slur/profanity list client- and server-side (so it can't be
  bypassed via devtools), nicknames are capped at 20 characters and only
  ever visible inside that one ephemeral room (never public/global), and
  the host can remove any player in two presses. There's no separate
  player-to-player reporting flow — the host-removal path is the
  moderation mechanism in place, same gap the Play checklist flagged for
  Android.
